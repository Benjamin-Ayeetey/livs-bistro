Before you
dig in.

How do I order?+

Choose a plate and build your meal enquiry. This preview uses sample details, so no order or payment is submitted. The live site can connect to the bistro’s ordering service.

Can you cater for a group?+

Use “Let’s plan a spread” to prepare an enquiry for your gathering. Share the occasion, number of guests, and what you have in mind.

What about dietary needs and allergies?+

Tell the kitchen about any allergies or dietary requirements before ordering. Ingredients and preparation need to be confirmed for each dish.

Where can I find you?+

We’re based in Accra, Ghana. Ask the bistro about its service area, delivery options, and opening hours.
